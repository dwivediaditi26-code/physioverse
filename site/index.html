Your patients' data, kept safe

Clinic records are sensitive. The app is built with that in mind.

  • Every user signs in with their own email and password
  • Role-based access: staff only see what their role allows
  • Data stored in a secure cloud database, available on every device
  • Automatic version history and daily backups
  • Download your own full backup any time
  • Activity log shows who changed what, and when
